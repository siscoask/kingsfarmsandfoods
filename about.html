About King's Farms and Foods

Discover our story, mission, and the passionate team behind your farm-fresh goods.

Our Story

King's Farms and Foods was founded in 2023 by Alade Omotayo David, a passionate farmer with a vision to bring sustainable, organic produce to Nigerian homes. Based in Lagos State, we started as a small family-run farm and have grown into a trusted source for farm-fresh goods, including organic vegetables, free-range poultry, and pure honey. Our commitment to quality and sustainability drives everything we do, ensuring that every product we deliver is grown with care and respect for the environment.

Our Mission & Vision

We strive to provide healthy, organic food while promoting sustainable farming practices and supporting local communities.

Our Mission

To deliver the freshest organic produce to your table, grown sustainably and ethically, while empowering local farmers.

Our Vision

To be Nigeria’s leading provider of organic, farm-fresh goods, fostering a healthier and more sustainable future.
