Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products and services

What areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across Lagos State and surrounding areas. For orders outside Lagos, please contact us on WhatsApp at +234 912 080 0600 to discuss delivery options. We're expanding our delivery network to serve more locations across Nigeria!

How fresh are your products?

All our products are harvested fresh from our farm daily. We guarantee farm-to-table freshness, with most produce delivered within 24-48 hours of harvest. Our eggs are collected daily from free-range hens, and honey is harvested seasonally and stored properly to maintain quality.

Are your products really organic?

Yes! We use 100% organic farming methods with no synthetic pesticides or chemical fertilizers. Our farm follows sustainable practices, and our poultry is free-range with natural feed. We're committed to providing pure, healthy produce for our customers.

What is your minimum order for delivery?

We offer free delivery for orders above ₦10,000 within Lagos. For orders below ₦10,000, a delivery fee applies based on your location. Contact us for specific delivery rates to your area.

How do I place an order?

You can place orders through our website by adding items to your cart and checking out, or contact us directly via WhatsApp at +234 912 080 0600. We also accept phone orders during business hours (Mon-Fri: 8am-5pm, Sat: 9am-3pm).

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers to our First Bank, Opay, and UBA accounts. After placing your order, you'll receive account details for payment. You can also pay on delivery for orders within Lagos (cash or bank transfer via mobile app).

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery within Lagos takes 1-2 business days. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 10am (additional charges may apply). For areas outside Lagos, delivery time varies from 2-5 business days depending on location.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale pricing?

Yes! We offer special wholesale prices for restaurants, hotels, and bulk buyers. Contact us at kingsfarms18@gmail.com or call +234 912 080 0600 to discuss your requirements and get a customized quote.

Can I visit your farm?

Absolutely! We welcome farm visits by appointment. It's a great way to see our organic farming practices firsthand and learn about sustainable agriculture. Contact us at least 2 days in advance to schedule your visit.

What if I'm not satisfied with my order?

Customer satisfaction is our priority. If you're not happy with your order, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery. We'll work with you to resolve the issue through replacement, refund, or credit for your next order.
