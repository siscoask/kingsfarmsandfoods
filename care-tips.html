Care & Tips

Learn how to keep your farm-fresh goods at their best with our expert advice!

Preserving Freshness

At King's Farm and Foods, we believe that proper care and storage are just as important as the quality of the produce itself. Our expert tips will help you maximize the shelf life, flavor, and nutritional value of your farm-fresh goods.

Tomatoes

Produce Storage

Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight. Avoid refrigerating to preserve flavor and texture.

Poultry

Poultry Safety

Refrigerate fresh poultry at 0-4°C and use within 1-2 days, or freeze for up to 6 months.

Dairy Products

Dairy Storage

Keep dairy products in the coldest part of your refrigerator. Never store milk in the door.

Leafy Greens

Produce Freshness

Wash greens just before use. Store unwashed in a breathable bag with a paper towel in the fridge.

Root Vegetables

Storage Produce

Store carrots, beets, and potatoes in a cool, dark, and well-ventilated place. Remove greens before storing.

Honey

Produce Storage

Store in a cool, dry place in a tightly sealed jar. Avoid heat and moisture to prevent crystallization.

How to Revive Wilted Greens

Produce Freshness Pro Tip

Place wilted leafy greens in a bowl of ice water for 15-30 minutes. They'll crisp right back up!

Expert Advice from Our Farm Team

"The key to freshness is understanding the unique needs of each type of produce. Never treat all fruits and vegetables the same way."

Temperature Control

Different products require different storage temperatures. Know what goes in the fridge and what doesn't.

Humidity Matters

Leafy greens need high humidity, while onions and garlic need dry conditions. Store accordingly.

Ethylene Sensitivity

Apples, bananas, and tomatoes produce ethylene gas that ripens other produce. Store them separately.

    Tomatoes

    Best Practices for Tomatoes

    Storage Temperature: 12-18°C (54-64°F)

    Refrigeration: Avoid if possible. Cold temperatures destroy flavor compounds and can make the texture mealy.

    Ripening: Green tomatoes can be ripened at room temperature in a paper bag with a banana to speed up the process.

    Shelf Life: 3-7 days at room temperature, 5-10 days if refrigerated (though flavor will be compromised).

    Pro Tip: If you must refrigerate tomatoes, take them out 30 minutes before eating to allow flavors to develop.
