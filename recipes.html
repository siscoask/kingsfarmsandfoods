    Spicy BBQ Chicken

    Prep Time 20 minutes
    Cook Time 1 hour
    Servings 4

    Ingredients

    • 4 chicken thighs
    • 2 tbsp olive oil
    • 1 tbsp paprika
    • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
    • 2 cloves garlic, minced
    • 1/4 cup BBQ sauce
    • Salt and pepper to taste
    • Fresh parsley for garnish

    Instructions

    1. In a bowl, mix olive oil, paprika, cayenne, garlic, salt, and pepper.
    2. Coat chicken thighs with the marinade and let sit for 1 hour.
    3. Preheat grill to medium-high heat.
    4. Grill chicken for 6-8 minutes per side, basting with BBQ sauce.
    5. Garnish with parsley and serve hot.
